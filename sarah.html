Absolute Data Sovereignty

We don't train models on your data. Your conversations, patient and customer names, and internal processes remain exclusively yours. Our systems are fine-tuned only on publicly available data. Your confidential information never leaves your local disk and is never used to improve global systems.

Protected IP & Licensing

Agent logic and unique code are our intellectual property. As part of the implementation fee, we grant you an exclusive license to use this system. While the data and work results belong to you, the agent core (its "brain" and integrations) remains protected by our copyrights. This model allows us to guarantee continuous optimization, security, and updates within your monthly service plan.

Zero-Footprint Guarantee

30-day deletion protocol. In case of contract termination, all local data and temporary configurations are securely deleted within 30 days. Upon request, we issue official confirmation of certified data destruction in compliance with the strictest security standards.
