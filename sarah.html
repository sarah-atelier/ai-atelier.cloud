Private Practice
The doctor examines the patient, speaks naturally, occasionally points to the X-ray monitor. The agent processes everything in real time in "shadow instances".

We don't train models on your data. Your conversations, patient and customer names, and internal processes remain exclusively yours. Our systems are fine-tuned only on publicly available data. Your confidential information never leaves your local disk and is never used to improve global systems.

Agent logic and unique code are our intellectual property. As part of the implementation fee, we grant you an exclusive license to use this system.
